# What the pilot already shows

Ten films is a test of the method, not a sample. Treat these as leads to check at 500.

1. **The mother is the hub.** A dead or dangerous mother appears in 6 of 10 films (Psycho, The Exorcist, Ringu, Get Out, Tumbbad, Hereditary). It is the most connected motif in the graph, ahead of every archetype.
2. **The deer.** Three films from three countries put a deer in the road as an early omen: Train to Busan (it rises from the dead in the first minutes), Get Out (the first jump scare, 10m20s) and Hereditary (Peter swerves to avoid one, then Charlie dies).
3. **Water carries the dead woman.** Ringu's well, La Llorona's pool and drowned children, Psycho's swamp. Okiku's well (1741) sits upstream of the same image.
4. **Day horror versus night horror.** Train to Busan is the brightest film by a margin (mean brightness 0.31 against roughly 0.19–0.21 for the rest). Nosferatu's tints say the same thing in 1922's language: about 22% of the film is tinted blue, the silent-era convention for night.
5. **Interiors.** Hereditary's screenplay is 83% interior scenes; Psycho's is 44%. Horror moving indoors, into the family, is a trend worth testing across the 500.
6. **Bias in the data itself.** Every film missing jump-scare and stills data except Nosferatu is non-Anglophone. Fan datasets reproduce the canon's blind spots.
