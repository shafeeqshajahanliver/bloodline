## Hardest cases

- **Where weakness ends and protection begins.** "Destroyed by fire" and "weak to sunlight" went to *it has a fatal weakness*, but "afraid of fire", "fire repels it" and "repelled by light" went to *faith and charms protect* (kind: light and fire hold it off), because they keep the threat back rather than end it. "Weak to stakes and holy water" went with the heart; "weak to salt, holy water, sunlight" went with holy water.
- **Escape: trapped versus pursued.** Generic "no escape" and "cannot escape" went to *trapped with no way out*, but "cannot be escaped by leaving", "cannot be outrun", "leaving does not help" and "distance buys time" (It Follows) went to *nothing can stop it*, because the threat follows you. The opposite values ("escape by leaving", "must leave immediately") were kept out of both, in *survivors can still win* (getting away saves you).
- **Looking.** "Must not look", "anyone who sees it dies" and "every animal has rules" (Nope: never meet its eye) are a rule-breaking fear, but "must not look away" means the opposite and has its own kind (keep your eyes on it) in the same fear; "spreads by eye contact" and "watching footage possesses" went to *it passes to others* because looking passes it on rather than breaking a rule.
- **Feeding on belief.** "Demon feeds on faith", "lives on belief", "fear gives the witch power" went to *it must feed* (it feeds on fear or belief), while "defeated by refusing fear" and "weakened by mockery" went to *survivors can still win*, since one says fear powers it and the other says courage beats it.
- **Games.** Saw-style values ("kill or family dies", "survive by self-mutilation", "own life against others") and "victims must take the bet" are deadly bargains, so they sit in *everything has a price* (games with deadly stakes), not in *calling it brings it*; but "turning around means yes" (Stree) and "victims must choose freely" went to *it must be let in* because they are about consent.
- **Inheritance versus contagion.** Family curses ("curse passes down bloodline", "kills each generation") are a kind inside *it passes to others* rather than a separate fear; "one must survive to continue" (We Are What We Are) and "women die in childbirth" (Purana Mandir) were judged family curses from their films.
- **Possession versus mind control.** "Possesses the living" is *it gets inside you* (takes over the body); "control through hair or belongings", "drugged food" (Rosemary's Baby), "love potion" and "toe rings bind women" (Bulbbul) were split between spells and mind control and silence enforced by threats, depending on whether the force is magic or people.
- **Unclear.** 44 values (2.7%) state a theme or slogan rather than a working rule, e.g. "based on real events", "death is a rebirth", "martyr means witness", "single unbroken take", "surgery as sex", "law of the strong".
