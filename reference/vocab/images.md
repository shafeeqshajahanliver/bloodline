## Hardest cases

- **Image versus setting of the image.** Many values name an image in a place ("dead cat in refrigerator", "corpse in a freezer", "blood on walls", "car sinking in pond"). They were grouped by the main thing shown (the dead cat, the corpse, the blood, the car in water), not by the container or place.
- **Animal sounds and absences.** A barking or howling animal stays with that animal ("howling dog" is a dog), but the absence of animals ("no birds", "no crickets", "silent birds") is grouped as silence, because what is noticed is the quiet.
- **Occult books versus books.** Books of magic or scripture ("book of spells", "bible", "book of the dead") go under holy and occult objects; books about a subject or stories ("book about vampires", "ghost story book", "book of the legend") stay with books.
- **Pendants.** A plain "pendant" (Rosemary's Baby, Braindead, The Descent) is jewellery; pendants named for a faith or charm ("ankh pendant", "om pendant", "saint pendant", "medallion") are charms.
- **Bodies with things inside them.** Implants, metal plates, rust spreading through flesh and a gun fused to a hand (Titane, Tetsuo, Crimes of the Future) form their own kind under changing bodies rather than machines.
- **Human figures versus monsters.** Human-shaped presences (faceless figures, doubles, ghost children) are ghosts and strange figures; anything with non-human anatomy, including werewolves and Silent Hill's "red pyramid", is a monster or creature.
- **Food and danger.** Razor blades in apples or sweets, rat droppings and maggots in food are "tainted food" rather than blades or vermin, because what is shown is food made unsafe.
- **Bags over heads versus masks.** A bag, cloth or plastic pulled over someone's head (suffocation, captivity) is grouped with restraints; a sack or burlap mask worn by a killer is a mask.
- **Vague or abstract values** went to `unclear` (9 values): "contagious evil", "promise to wait forever", "rejected proposal", "revenge as a river", "sign language", "white border", and three dreams.
