## Hardest cases

- **person who died wronged / people who died wronged / person wronged / wronged woman**: kept apart from "evil outliving death". Wronged victims (Imhotep, the Elizabeth Dane crew) are separated from the already-wicked dead (Krueger, Asa, Báthory). Executions went with the wicked dead because the executed figures in the examples are witches and killers, though some (executed heretics) could be read as wronged.
- **inherited**: the example (the devil claims Lucy's daughter) mixes a pact with inheritance. Grouped under family blood, since the value itself names inheritance; "pact for a child" stays under bargains.
- **unexplained** (50 films): left as its own fear, "no known source", rather than `unclear`, because it is a real answer (the story gives no origin), not an unplaceable value.
- **accident** (Eyes Without a Face, The Hands of Orlac): placed under the body (injury), not under science, because the value names the accident, not the surgery that followed.
- **born from a pregnancy / born of evil / spirit possessed unborn child**: placed with the body and birth rather than family blood, since the fear is the unnatural birth, not a family line.
- **made by a demon** (Nosferatu: lineage from Belial) went under unnatural births; **made by a curse** and **made by black magic** went under curses, not under "monsters people made", which is kept for science and industry.
- **released by accident** (28 Days Later) went to disease spreading; **opened by accident** (The Beyond) to an opened door to hell.
- **failed harvest** (Children of the Corn) went under a cruel society (society collapsing), though it could be read as a cult origin.
- **residual energy** (Hell House) went under places with dark pasts, as a force left in the house.
- **war wound** (Jacob's Ladder) and **terminally ill man** (Saw) went under illness or injury rather than war or the mind.
