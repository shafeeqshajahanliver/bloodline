# Trigger: what sets the story off

Grouped from `reference/vocab/input/trigger.tsv` (594 values). 26 fears, 117 kinds.

## making a new home

Settling into a new house, town or institution, the place that will turn on you.

Kinds: moving into a house; inheriting a house; fixing up an old place; moving to a new place; joining a school or institution.

## going back home

Returning to a home, birthplace or family, or a loved one coming back to you.

Kinds: returning home; returning to one's roots; visiting family; a loved one comes back.

## travelling somewhere remote

Setting out on a journey, trip or stay that takes people far from help.

Kinds: a journey; a trip into nature; staying somewhere isolated; a trip to an event; sent on a mission.

## cut off and trapped

Breaking down, getting lost, being stranded, isolated or confined.

Kinds: vehicle breaks down; getting lost; stranded with no way back; left alone or isolated; seeking shelter; waking up confined.

## an outsider reaches in

A stranger, newcomer, intruder, double or unexpected call enters ordinary life.

Kinds: a stranger comes to town; taking in a stranger; a knock at the door; meeting a stranger; new neighbours arrive; an intruder breaks in; someone not who they seem; a call or message.

## saying yes to an offer

Accepting an invitation, job, role, contract or membership that turns out badly.

Kinds: accepting an invitation; taking a job; landing a role; signing a contract; joining a group.

## bringing something home

Taking, receiving, finding or handling an object that carries the harm.

Kinds: taking an object; receiving or buying an object; finding a hidden object; handling a cursed object; opening a cursed object.

## unsealing what was shut away

Opening graves, tombs, sealed rooms or cages, or handling the dead.

Kinds: disturbing a grave; unearthing something buried; opening a sealed room; finding or handling a body; releasing a captive.

## breaking a rule

Ignoring a warning, breaking a taboo, trespassing or deliberately provoking it.

Kinds: ignoring a warning; breaking a taboo; trespassing; entering an abandoned place; straying from the path; provoking the threat.

## calling on unseen powers

Seances, summonings, spirit games, spells, pacts and prophecies.

Kinds: holding a seance; summoning a spirit; a game turns real; reading forbidden words; working magic or ritual; making a pact or wish; seeking a prophecy; visiting a sacred place.

## digging into a mystery

Investigating, searching out a legend, or filming in pursuit of the truth.

Kinds: investigating a crime or disappearance; investigating a haunting; chasing a legend or cult; investigating an object; filming for an audience.

## someone goes missing

A person is taken or vanishes, or a relative goes searching for them.

Kinds: someone is taken; someone vanishes; searching for a missing relative.

## losing family

A death, suicide or breakdown in the family, or a new parent or child arriving.

Kinds: death of a parent; death of a child; grief for a loved one; a sudden unexplained death; suicide of someone close; a family breaking apart; a new parent or child.

## seeing or hearing too much

## strange signs and visions

## a sudden accident or attack

## the body changing

## playing god

## disaster strikes

## running from trouble

Kinds: fleeing danger or punishment; losing one's home; trying to leave.

## need and greed

Kinds: poverty and want; chasing money or fame.

## being spurned or shamed

Kinds: romantic rejection; refusing a request; humiliation and cruel pranks; blame and injustice; betrayal.

## a secret surfaces

Kinds: an anniversary or festival; a party; a wedding or funeral.

## love and desire

## unclear

## Hardest cases

- **invitation, jobs, roles** ("an invitation", "taking a job", 20+ films): could sit with travel since most lead to a remote house. Grouped as *saying yes to an offer* because the act that sets things off is agreeing, not the travel itself.
- **"a newcomer buys the house", "new family moves in"**: could be about outsiders arriving next door; kept under *making a new home* since the newcomers are the ones the story follows. "new neighbour moves in" stays under *an outsider reaches in*.
- **"secret hidden" and "faked death"** sit beside "secret discovered" in *a secret surfaces*, but in their own kind (*a secret is hidden*) since hiding and revealing are opposite acts.
- **"saying a name" / "saying the name"**: could be breaking a rule; placed under summoning because saying the name calls the threat.
- **"rejecting the supernatural"** (burning protective charms): placed with ignoring a warning, not with calling on powers, since the act is throwing away protection.
- **"witnessing a suicide"** goes with witnessing violence, while "friend's suicide" and "spouse's suicide" go under losing family: the first is about seeing, the others about loss.
- **"stillbirth"** under death of a child rather than pregnancy and birth.
- **"isolated retreat"** (staying at a cabin to quit heroin) under staying somewhere isolated, not drugs.
- **unclear** (5 values, under 1%): sabotage, self-sacrifice gone wrong, unexplained, noticed by the threat, drawing a picture.
