## Hardest cases

- **the self** (44 films, the most common value): kept with the mind-based values in "the enemy within" (delusion, split personality, guilt), not with "the body turning monstrous", although some of its films (Jekyll and Hyde, The Hands of Orlac) involve bodily change. The value as written names the person, not their body.
- **werewolf / werewolves / wolf / shapeshifter**: placed under "the body turning monstrous" (a person becomes a beast) rather than "monsters and creatures", because the details stress being bitten and transforming. Shapeshifters with no human self (It, shape-shifting entity) stay with creatures; ones that copy people (The Thing) go to "impostors and doubles".
- **cannibal hunger** (Raw, Bones and All): an urge inside the main characters, so "the enemy within", not "people who kill" with the cannibal families.
- **avengers** (avenger, vengeful parents, wronged woman, vengeful victim) are living people, so "people who kill" (kind "human avengers"); vengeful spirits of the dead stay in "the restless dead".
- **hunters of the accused**: witch hunters and witch-hunting authorities go to "the powerful and their systems"; vampire hunters, vigilante groups and the superstitious relative to "the group turns on you". They are the persecutors, never grouped with the witches or vampires they hunt.
- **obsession** (Shankar's fixation) and the obsessed collector/director/fan are treated as dangerous obsessive people ("people who kill", kind "obsessive"), not as the main character's own mind.
- **invisible stalker** (The Invisible Man, 2020) is the abusive ex-partner, so "people in your own life" rather than "something unseen".
- **monster from a book** (The Babadook) is grouped as a monster from story/folklore, although the film reads it as grief; the separate value "grief" carries that reading.
- **possessed objects** (Chucky, the ventriloquist's dummy, the haunted mirror) sit with cursed objects under "witchcraft and cursed things", not "being taken over", which is kept for people and body parts.
- **intruders** (The Others) and **her own death**: the twist is that the characters are dead, so "death and fate".
- **broadcast signal / violent media / language virus**: Videodrome's signal and violent media are "cursed or deadly media"; spreading words, hysteria and suicide epidemics are "spreading idea" under "sickness and poison".
- **anonymous messenger** (Personal Shopper): could be a ghost or a living stalker; left as `unclear`.
