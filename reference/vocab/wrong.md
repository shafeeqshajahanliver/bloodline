## Hardest cases

1. **Victims who are both children and family, or both children and sexual-assault victims.** Every wrong whose victim is a child (parent kills child, child raped, child sacrifice aside) goes under *harm done to children*, so *killing within the family* and *sexual violation* cover adult victims only. Child sacrifice stays under *human sacrifice* because the ritual is the point.
2. **Blame versus loss.** Values with no wrongdoer (death of a child, death of a spouse, husband taken by war, unresolved grief, suicides) are in *loss that won't heal*; accidental deaths with someone at fault or hiding it (hit and run, child killed in accident, parent's accidental harm) are in *an accident and its guilt*; preventable deaths through inaction (failure to help, negligence, cowardice) are in *failing to protect*.
3. **Captivity.** imprisonment, torture of a servant (Bramayugam), kidnapping (Apostle) and relative committed (Next of Kin) went to *the powerful abuse their power* as 'held captive', not to cruelty at home, because each is one party exercising power over another's freedom.
4. **Spiritual binding as exploitation.** enslaving a god (Apostle) and imprisoning a summoned spirit (Noroi) are under *exploitation for profit* (slavery) rather than *sacred rules broken*, since the wrong is using a being for gain.
5. **Smaller judgement calls.** cover-up by parents (Prom Night-style concealment) sits with institutional cover-ups; grandmother offers family (Hereditary) is a pact with evil; failed exorcism (Qodrat) is under defying the divine; stolen identity (Us, The Lighthouse) is a secret kept; men used as bait is a failure to protect; abandoning an identity (Perfect Blue) is under selfish appetites as giving up oneself, the weakest fit.
