# who_suffers: fears and kinds

Where a value names both a role and a situation ("grieving mother", "pregnant sister", "captive daughter", "abused wife"), the situation decides the fear, because it is what the story uses to make that person vulnerable.

## Hardest cases

- **grief, pregnancy, abuse or captivity versus family role**: values like "grieving mother", "pregnant widow", "captive daughter" and "abused wife" were placed by situation, not relation. "pregnant widow" (Inside) went to expectant parents rather than the grieving because the pregnancy drives the film.
- **the killer himself, the self, the scientist himself, the woman herself**: split by what happens. Those who turn into the threat (An American Werewolf in London, Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde, Corazon) are in *those consumed from within*; those that already are the monster and suffer as such (Cat People, Under the Skin's predator, The Lure) are in *monsters who suffer*; killers destroyed by ghosts or others (Raat, 13B) are in *the guilty punished*. "the killer himself" (Peeping Tom, Session 9) went to self-destroying minds.
- **women, women he desires, lookalike women**: the generic "women" (Peeping Tom, Martin, Tenebrae) are women hunted by a male killer, so they sit with *objects of desire*; "young woman" and "young women" stay under teenagers and young adults because their examples (Caligari, Dracula) give no single situation.
- **lonely men and seduced men** (Under the Skin, Veerana) are lured victims, not lonely people, so they are in *the trapped and the surviving*.
- **young criminals, young burglars, intruders, gang members** were placed with *the guilty punished* (criminals); in some films (Frontier(s)) they are closer to ordinary victims.
- **nobles and wealthy clients** became a small kind, *the privileged*, inside whole communities rather than a fear of their own.
- **cult members** (cult, cult leader, insider wanting out, young initiates) went to whole communities as *cults and sects*.
- **mistaken victim** and **targets** are too vague to place and are `unclear` (2 of 751).
